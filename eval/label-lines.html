Ground truth for auto-rectify. On each stitched panorama, click along a painted line that should be straight after rectifying: for example the lower edge of the red rim band, a glyph-band frame line, or the top of the base band. Click every few centimetres across the whole width, following the line through any steps between stitched pieces. New line starts another line (2 per panorama is ideal: one near the top, one near the bottom). Drag points to adjust. Labels are saved in this browser; use Copy labels and paste them into the chat.
